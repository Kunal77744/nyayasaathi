RESIDENTIAL RENT AGREEMENT (SAMPLE, REVISED DRAFT)

This Rent Agreement is made on 1 October 2026 between Mr. Ramesh Sharma (the "Landlord") and Ms. Anita Verma (the "Tenant") for the flat at 204, Green View Apartments, Dwarka, New Delhi.

1. Rent. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000 on or before the 10th of every month. A late fee of Rs. 200 per day applies after the 10th, capped at Rs. 2,000 per month.

2. Security deposit. The Tenant has paid a security deposit of Rs. 50,000 (two months' rent). The Landlord shall refund the deposit within 30 days of the Tenant vacating the flat, after deducting only documented damages beyond normal wear and tear, with receipts shared with the Tenant.

3. Term and lock-in. The tenancy is for 11 months from 1 October 2026. There is a lock-in period of 6 months. If the Tenant leaves during the lock-in, one month's rent will be deducted from the security deposit.

4. Rent increase. Rent may be increased by up to 5% only at the time of renewal, with 60 days' written notice.

5. Notice period. Either party may end this agreement by giving one month's written notice after the lock-in period.

6. Repairs. The Landlord shall bear the cost of structural repairs and major plumbing or electrical faults. The Tenant shall bear minor repairs up to Rs. 2,000 per incident.

7. Entry. The Landlord may enter the flat for inspection or repairs only after giving 24 hours' notice, at reasonable hours.

8. Subletting. The Tenant shall not sublet the flat. Guests and immediate family may stay.

9. Termination by Landlord. The Landlord may terminate this agreement before the end of the term only for non-payment of rent for two consecutive months or serious breach, after giving one month's notice.

10. Disputes. Any dispute shall first be discussed between the parties for 30 days. Courts at Delhi shall have jurisdiction. Each party shall bear its own legal costs.

Signed by both parties on the date written above.
