RESIDENTIAL RENT AGREEMENT (SAMPLE)

This Rent Agreement is made on 1 October 2026 between Mr. Ramesh Sharma (the "Landlord") and Ms. Anita Verma (the "Tenant") for the flat at 204, Green View Apartments, Dwarka, New Delhi.

1. Rent. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000 on or before the 5th of every month. A late fee of Rs. 500 per day applies after the 5th.

2. Security deposit. The Tenant has paid a security deposit of Rs. 75,000 (three months' rent). The deposit is refundable when the Tenant vacates the flat. The Landlord may deduct any amount he considers necessary for damages or dues.

3. Term and lock-in. The tenancy is for 11 months from 1 October 2026. There is a lock-in period of 11 months. If the Tenant leaves before the lock-in ends, the entire security deposit will be forfeited.

4. Rent increase. The Landlord may increase the rent by up to 15% at any time by giving 7 days' written notice.

5. Notice period. Either party may end this agreement by giving one month's written notice after the lock-in period.

6. Repairs. The Tenant shall bear the cost of all repairs and maintenance, including structural repairs and plumbing or electrical faults, whatever the cause.

7. Entry. The Landlord or his representatives may enter the flat at any time without prior notice for inspection.

8. Subletting. The Tenant shall not sublet or share the flat with anyone other than immediate family.

9. Termination by Landlord. The Landlord may terminate this agreement at any time by giving the Tenant three months' notice, and the Tenant shall vacate the flat within that period.

10. Disputes. Any dispute shall be settled by the courts at Delhi. The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord's legal costs in any dispute.

Signed by both parties on the date written above.
